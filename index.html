Guest menu

What to do around Fort Myers.

This guide gives guests a quick menu of local activities, food, shopping, and fishing ideas. It can be expanded into an automated itinerary for each future location.

Beaches and outdoors

  • Fort Myers Beach for sand, sunsets, casual food, and Gulf water.
  • Sanibel and Captiva for shelling, wildlife, bikes, and slower island days.
  • Lovers Key State Park for kayaking, beach walks, and nature trails.
  • Edison and Ford Winter Estates for history, gardens, and a quieter afternoon.

Fishing and water days

  • Backwater fishing charters for snook, redfish, trout, and tarpon season planning.
  • Deep-sea fishing options from Fort Myers Beach and nearby marinas.
  • Guest-use basic fishing equipment available with the unit.
  • Local license, charter, tide, and weather rules should be checked before fishing.

Entertainment

  • Downtown Fort Myers River District for restaurants, bars, music, and events.
  • Spring training baseball in season with the Red Sox and Twins nearby.
  • Key West Express from Fort Myers Beach for a bigger day-trip option.
  • Boat tours, dolphin cruises, sunset cruises, and eco tours.

Food spots to research first

  • Downtown River District seafood and oyster bars.
  • Waterfront dining near Fort Myers Beach and Cape Coral.
  • Sanibel and Captiva island restaurants for beach-day meals.
  • Casual breakfast cafes, taco spots, and family-friendly patios.

Shopping

  • Bell Tower Shops for dining, shopping, and easy errands.
  • Miromar Outlets in Estero for outlet shopping.
  • Gulf Coast Town Center for restaurants, stores, and entertainment.
  • Downtown boutiques and local markets for smaller local finds.

Three-day sample plan

  • Day 1: Arrive, stock groceries, River District dinner.
  • Day 2: Beach or Sanibel shelling, seafood dinner, sunset.
  • Day 3: Fishing charter or Edison/Ford Estates, shopping, relaxed night in.
  • Departure: Cleaning scheduled after checkout.
Travel from Western New York

Start with Niagara Falls to Punta Gorda on Allegiant.

For Buffalo-area guests, the preferred low-fare strategy is to pre-book Allegiant Air from Niagara Falls International Airport (IAG) to Punta Gorda Airport (PGD), then arrange a rideshare, shuttle, rental car, or private car to Fort Myers.

Niagara Falls/Buffalo to Punta Gorda Airfare estimate Allegiant advertises low base fares that can start around $45 one-way on select dates. Plan early and verify baggage, seat, and schedule costs directly with Allegiant.
Punta Gorda Airport (PGD) to Fort Myers Ground estimate Rideshare often plans around $40-$70. Pre-arranged car service commonly starts around $70-$80+ depending on destination and vehicle.
Southwest Florida International (RSW) to Fort Myers Backup airport estimate Rideshare or taxi commonly plans around $25-$45 to central Fort Myers, with higher fares to beaches or islands.
Rental car Recommended for exploring Many beaches, restaurants, shopping centers, and fishing docks are spread out. A rental car gives guests the most flexibility.

Travel costs are planning estimates only. Airline fares, baggage fees, rideshare surge pricing, tolls, taxes, and seasonal demand change frequently.
