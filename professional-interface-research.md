## 🎨 UI/UX INDUSTRY STANDARDS

### **Visual Hierarchy Patterns:**
1. **Header Navigation** (tabs/menu)
2. **Primary Content Area** (commands)
3. **Secondary Information** (descriptions)
4. **Status/Feedback** (copy confirmations)

### **Color Psychology in Professional Tools:**
- **Blue (#007acc)**: Trust, reliability, professional
- **Green (#27ae60)**: Success, positive actions
- **Red (#e74c3c)**: Warnings, destructive actions
- **Yellow (#f39c12)**: Attention, important info
- **Purple (#9b59b6)**: Advanced/premium features

### **Spacing and Typography:**
- **12-16px**: Body text
- **18-24px**: Headings
- **Monospace fonts**: Code/commands
- **8-16px**: Padding/margins
- **1.4-1.6**: Line height for readability

---

## 🚨 COMMON DESIGN MISTAKES TO AVOID

### **From Our Current Implementation:**
1. **Inconsistent "Read More" application** - Some commands missing
2. **Inadequate visual feedback** - Small checkmarks on high-res
3. **Layout contrast issues** - Multi-command boxes awkward
4. **Information overload** - Too many details in small space

### **Industry Anti-Patterns:**
1. **Feature creep** - Adding too many options
2. **Inconsistent interaction models** - Different click behaviors
3. **Poor mobile/scaling** - Not considering different screen sizes
4. **Lack of progressive disclosure** - Showing all details at once
