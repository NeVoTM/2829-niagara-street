## STRUCTURAL SYSTEM

### Cast-in-Place Post-Tensioned Concrete

**Advantages for Mid-Rise:**
- Faster floor cycles (4-5 days vs 7-10 for conventional)
- Thinner slabs (7-8" vs 10-12")
- Longer spans without drop beams
- Reduced cracking and deflection
- Better for coastal exposure (reduced permeability)

**System Description:**
- Post-tensioned flat plate slabs, 7.5" typical
- PT tendons, unbonded, Grade 270 ksi
- Shear walls and cores for lateral system
- Perimeter spandrel beams as needed
- f'c = 5,000 psi typical, 6,000 psi for columns

**Unit Costs (Miami-Dade, 2025):**
- PT slab system: $18-22/SF (in-place, incl formwork, rebar, PT, concrete)
- Columns: $450-600/CY
- Shear walls: $400-500/CY
- Foundations (mat or piles): $85-110/SF

**Forming System:**
- Doka or Peri flying tables (rental)
- 4-5 day cycle per floor
- Reshoring 2 floors below
- Early-strip capability with cylinder breaks

## ENVELOPE & WATERPROOFING

### High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) Compliance

**Glazing:**
- Impact-resistant glazing per Miami-Dade NOA
- 1" insulated units, Low-E, laminated outer lite
- Aluminum curtain wall or storefront, tested to ±105 psf
- Cost: $85-110/SF including installation

**Waterproofing:**
- Below-grade: Bentonite or torch-down membrane
- Podium deck: Hot rubberized asphalt, traffic coating
- Rooftop: Modified bitumen, white TPO reflective
- Pool deck: Liquid-applied membrane under finishes
- Cost: $8-15/SF depending on system

**Roofing:**
- TPO single-ply, white, 80 mil, fully adhered
- R-30 polyiso insulation
- Cost: $12-16/SF

## MEP SYSTEMS

### Plumbing
- Fixtures: 2.5 per unit (residential), 15 total (Chabad), 6 (gym/pool)
- Domestic water: 3/4" to 1-1/2" copper or PEX
- Waste: PVC, ABS, or cast iron risers
- Hot water: Central gas or electric, tankless or storage
- Cost: $8-12/SF

### HVAC
- Mini-split heat pumps per unit (SEER 16+)
- Central chiller for common areas (optional)
- Ventilation: ERV or HRV for each unit
- Tonnage: 400-500 SF/ton (Miami climate)
- Cost: $12-18/SF

### Fire Protection
- Wet-pipe sprinkler system throughout
- NFPA 13 for residential occupancy
- Standpipe and hose connections
- Fire pump if pressure insufficient
- Cost: $6-9/SF

### Electrical
- 200-400A service per unit
- Main service: 2000-3000A, 277/480V transformer
- Emergency generator: 250-350 kW
- EV charger capacity: 360 kW additional
- LED lighting throughout
- Cost: $14-20/SF
