## ⚠️ CRITICAL ISSUES

### 1. Project is Underwater by $8.3M

**The project loses money at $1,000/SF pricing.**

To break even, prices would need to be approximately **$1,169/SF** (17% increase).

### 2. Cost Overruns vs Baseline

| Category | Baseline | Detailed Estimate | Variance |

**Key Drivers:**

- Revenue at $1,000/SF: $49.5M

- **Shortfall: $8.3M (-14.4%)**

## 🔧 RECOMMENDATIONS TO ACHIEVE FEASIBILITY

### Path 1: Increase Revenue (+37%)

**Target:** $1,369/SF average  
**Achievable through:**
- Market positioning as luxury product
- Enhanced finishes & amenities
- Premium waterfront location
- Religious community demand premium

**Risk:** High - Market may not support pricing

### Path 2: Reduce Costs (-25%)

**Target:** $43.3M total costs  
**Achievable through:**

**Hard Cost Reductions ($10M target):**
- Reduce contingencies from 16% to 8% (-$4.4M)
- Value engineer finishes (-$2.5M)
- Competitive bidding for GC O&P (-$1.5M)
- Optimize parking (surface vs structured) (-$1.0M)
- Material buy-outs & early procurement (-$0.6M)

**Soft Cost Reductions ($3.7M target):**
- Reduce A&E fees to 3.5% (-$600K)
- Negotiate financing to 7% rate (-$450K)
- Self-perform marketing (-$400K)
- Reduce developer fee (-$1.8M)
- Challenge impact fees (-$200K)
- Owner-builder savings (-$250K)

**Risk:** Moderate - Quality & schedule impacts

### Path 3: Hybrid Approach (RECOMMENDED)

**Increase Revenue:** +15% to $1,150/SF = $56.9M  
**Reduce Costs:** -12% to $50.8M  
**Result:** $6.1M profit (10.7% margin, 12.0% ROI)

**Implementation:**
1. **Design Phase (6 months)**
   - Value engineering workshops
   - Competitive GC selection
   - Material pre-purchasing
   - Target: 8% cost reduction

2. **Pre-Sales Phase (6 months)**
   - Premium positioning
   - Early buyer incentives
   - 50% pre-sales required
   - Target: $1,150/SF average

3. **Construction (24 months)**
   - Fixed-price GMP contract
   - Milestone-based draws
   - Contingency management
   - Change order control

## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

1. **The $1,000/SF target is not achievable** with current cost structure
2. **Baseline budget was understated** by ~$10M (+22%)
3. **Financing costs are significant** at $2.6M (need to reduce LTC or rate)
4. **Contingencies are high but appropriate** for budget phase
5. **Market pricing must support $1,150-1,200/SF** for viability
6. **Chabad/Mikvah at-cost impacts margins** but provides community value

## ⚖️ GO/NO-GO DECISION FACTORS

### ✅ GO IF:
- Market supports $1,150+/SF pricing
- Can reduce costs by 10-15% through VE
- Strong pre-sales (50%+ at target prices)

- Cannot achieve cost reductions
- Pre-sales weak or slow
