## 🎯 **PROJECT OVERVIEW**

**Warp Assistant** transforms Warp AI from a chat interface into a **persistent, intelligent programming companion** that remembers your preferences, projects, and patterns. No more rebuilding relationships with AI every session.

### **Core Philosophy:**
> *"Focus on creating, not memorizing. The AI handles syntax, you handle vision."*
